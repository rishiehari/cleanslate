Common questions

Did CleanSlate delete something I wanted? Open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted. Items stay there for 30 days and can be recovered.

Why didn't it find copies that are in iCloud only? It can only compare originals that are on your iPhone. Turn on "iCloud originals" in the app menu to let it download them (Wi-Fi only, within a limit).

How do I free the space right away? Empty Recently Deleted in the Photos app.
