Privacy policy

Draft for the owner's review. Last updated: 2026-11-06.

CleanSlate collects no data. It has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

What the app does with your photos

With your permission, CleanSlate reads your photo library on your iPhone to find duplicate and similar photos, videos and screenshots. Analysis happens on the device. Photos, thumbnails and the results never leave your iPhone.

The app keeps a small index on your iPhone (photo identifiers, dates, file names and numeric fingerprints) so scans can resume quickly. It is excluded from device backups and can be erased from the app menu ("Clear saved scan data"). Deleting the app removes it.

Optional: checking iCloud originals

If you turn on "Check iCloud originals", CleanSlate asks Apple's Photos framework to download originals that are stored only in iCloud, over Wi-Fi only and within a limit you choose, so it can compare them. The downloads come from Apple's iCloud to your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Deleting photos

CleanSlate can only delete photos by asking Apple's Photos system, which always shows its own confirmation. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is on, deletions sync to your other devices.

Testers

In TestFlight builds, a tester can turn on "Label mode" and export anonymous labels (random hashes and numbers; no images, identifiers, dates or locations) and share them manually. Nothing is sent automatically.
