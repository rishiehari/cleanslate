CleanSlate helps you free up space on your iPhone without losing a memory.

It finds identical copies, bursts and similar shots, duplicate videos, old screenshots and screen recordings, and your biggest videos, and shows you exactly what you'd free up.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• Review each group, tap what to keep, then Approve. Approving only adds photos to your tray.
• Nothing is deleted until you confirm in CleanSlate, and again in Apple's own prompt.
• Favorites and photos in your albums are never offered for deletion.
• Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking, no uploads.
• Free, with no subscriptions.

HONEST NUMBERS
• You see verified file sizes, clearly separated from estimates.
• A history shows what was deleted and your free space before and after.

Tip: Apple's Photos app (Collections, Utilities, Duplicates) can merge exact copies. CleanSlate adds bursts and similar shots, videos, screenshots and large files.
